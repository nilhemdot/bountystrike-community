# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

> **Status: ASPIRATIONAL.** This document states the intended contributor terms.
> It is **not yet enforced** — no automated DCO check or signing mechanism is wired.
> Wiring enforcement (DCO sign-off bot / CLA-assistant) is a later plan. Until then,
> this is a statement of intent, not a binding gate.

## Terms (intended)

By contributing to BountyStrike, a contributor agrees that:

1. **Grant.** The contributor grants BountyStrike a perpetual, worldwide,
   non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, modify, and distribute
   their contribution.

2. **Relicensing within the three-tier model.** The contributor grants BountyStrike
   the right to license their contribution under any of the project's tier licenses
   (Apache-2.0 for core, AGPL-3.0 for community, or the proprietary enterprise
   license), consistent with the tier the contribution lands in and the relicensing
   boundary in `LICENSE`.

3. **Originality.** The contribution is the contributor's own work, or they have the
   right to submit it under these terms.

4. **No warranty.** Contributions are provided "as is".

## Future enforcement (deferred)

- DCO `Signed-off-by:` trailer required on commits, OR
- CLA-assistant bot gating PRs.

Tracked as a Phase 0/1 governance task.
